Privacy Policy

Trace · Last updated: 23 June 2026

Trace ("the App", "we", "our") is a habit-tracking application developed by Marko Kosta. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

Trace is designed to work entirely offline. All data is stored locally on your device only. We collect the following information:

  • Your first name — entered voluntarily during onboarding, used only to personalise the in-app voice companion. Never transmitted off-device.
  • Habit data — the habits you create, your completion history, streaks, and notes. Stored in a local SQLite database on your device.
  • App preferences — theme selection, voice settings, and notification preferences. Stored locally using Android's AsyncStorage.
  • Motion sensor data — the App reads your device's accelerometer and pedometer to detect whether you are moving or stationary. This data is processed in real-time in memory only and is never stored or transmitted.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect any personally identifiable information beyond your first name.
  • We do not collect your location, precise or otherwise.
  • We do not have analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not use any backend servers. There is no account creation and no data is ever sent over the internet.
  • We do not share any data with third parties.

3. Device Permissions

The App requests the following Android permissions:

  • ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION — used to count steps via the pedometer sensor. No step data is transmitted off-device.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS — used to send optional daily reminders and streak-risk alerts that you configure. You can disable these at any time in Android Settings.
  • VIBRATE — used for haptic feedback when completing habits.

4. Text-to-Speech

The voice companion feature uses your device's built-in Android Text-to-Speech engine. No audio is recorded, transmitted, or stored. The feature can be disabled at any time in the App's Settings screen.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

All your data lives exclusively on your device. You can permanently delete all data at any time by using the Factory Reset option in the App's Settings screen, or by uninstalling the App. Uninstalling the App deletes all locally stored habit data.

You can export your data at any time using the Export All Data option in Settings.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be reflected on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
